      AI LAB

      How AI and bots work

      A short lesson for a curious kid. Then build a better request, and take a tiny quiz.

      What is AI?

      AI means artificial intelligence. People built it. It is not a kid, not a grown-up, and not magic. It is good at spotting patterns — like noticing that the word "goal" shows up in soccer stories — and using those patterns to help with a task.

      How a chatbot answers

      A chatbot reads your message, looks for clues, and guesses a helpful reply from examples it learned. It can sound friendly. It still does not know your life, and it is not your friend.

      1. You typeA question or a job for the bot.
      2. CluesIt notices the topic, the task, and how hard you want it.
      3. A guessIt builds a reply one piece at a time from patterns.
      4. You decideUse it, ask a follow-up, or check with a grown-up.

      Write a good request

      Clear and specific beats one mystery word. Say what you want, add details you care about, and ask a follow-up if the first answer is too easy, too long, or confusing.

      Try: "Give me a hard multiplication puzzle about basketball. Wait for my answer, then tell me if I got it."

      Bots can be wrong

      A bot can sound sure and still be wrong. It can mix up a score, invent a fact, or use old news. If it matters — homework, safety, a real rule — check it and ask a grown-up. You are the captain. The bot is a helper.

      Online safety

      Never share your full name with a bot or a website.
      Never share your address.
      Never share your school.
      Never share passwords. Not even "just to check."
      Favorites are fine: dogs, sports, colors, math, jokes.
      If a page asks for a secret, stop and tell a grown-up.
